# BROKEYDOBKEY — VISUAL BRAND GUIDELINES
## The Designer-Free Design System

> **The rule:** If you can't build it in Canva using these guidelines, it shouldn't exist. Consistency beats creativity. Anyone on the team should be able to produce on-brand content without asking permission.

---

## 1. COLOUR PALETTE

### Primary Colours

| Name | Hex | RGB | Usage |
|------|-----|-----|-------|
| **BrokeyPink** | `#E8457C` | 232, 69, 124 | PRIMARY brand colour. Hero backgrounds, header blocks, main CTA buttons. This is the brand. |
| **BrokeyOrange** | `#F5A623` | 245, 166, 35 | Accent colour. Used on key words ("Earn"), highlights, price callouts, hover states. |
| **DarkPurple** | `#1A0A2E` | 26, 10, 46 | Logo colour, dark backgrounds, LOGIN-style buttons, footer. |
| **White** | `#ffffff` | 255, 255, 255 | Text on pink/dark, button text, breathing room, card backgrounds. |

### Secondary Colours (Use sparingly — one per piece of content max)

| Name | Hex | Usage |
|------|-----|-------|
| **MutedPurple** | `#7B4F8E` | Secondary buttons ("Recycle & earn £30" style), tags, pill labels |
| **LightPink** | `#FDF0F5` | Subtle section backgrounds, card fills, email backgrounds |
| **CircuitGreen** | `#4ade80` | Success states, environmental stats, verified badges |
| **SlateGrey** | `#6b7280` | Subtext, secondary labels, metadata |

### Colour Rules

1. **BrokeyPink is your hero.** It goes on backgrounds, hero sections, headers. Everything else serves it.
2. **BrokeyOrange is for one word or one number per piece.** It draws the eye. If everything is orange, nothing is.
3. **White text on pink always.** Never dark text on pink — it muddies the energy.
4. **DarkPurple for authority.** Logo, nav, login buttons, footers. It grounds the pink.
5. **Never use gradients.** Flat colour only. If you're tempted to use a gradient, don't.
6. **The tagline is always:** "Don't bin it. Earn from it." — "Earn" in BrokeyOrange, everything else in white or dark.

## 2. TYPOGRAPHY

### Font Stack

| Font | Use Case | Weight | Style |
|------|----------|--------|-------|
| **Space Grotesk** | Headlines, hero text, product names, any statement copy | 700 (Bold), 800 (Black) | UPPERCASE always for H1/H2. Mixed case acceptable for H3/body headers. |
| **Chakra Petch** | Numbers, stats, prices, tech specs, badges, countdown timers | 600, 700 | Uppercase. The "tech" font. Never use for long paragraphs. |
| **Inter** | Body copy, descriptions, captions, form labels, long reads | 400 (Regular), 500 (Medium) | Sentence case. Readable. Not shouted. |
| **Monospace (system)** | Code, terminal-style callouts, error messages, joke "system" text | 400 | Use for flavour — "PROCESSING YOUR JUNK..." type moments |

### Typography Hierarchy

```
H1 — Space Grotesk, 700-800, UPPERCASE, 48-72px
     Example: "DON'T BIN IT. EARN FROM IT."

H2 — Space Grotesk, 700, UPPERCASE, 32-40px
     Example: "YOUR BROKEN PS4 IS WORTH MORE THAN YOU THINK"

H3 — Space Grotesk, 600, Mixed case, 24-28px
     Example: "Here's what repair shops actually pay"

Body — Inter, 400, Sentence case, 16-18px, line-height 1.6
     Example: "The broken tech market in the UK is worth an estimated..."

Caption / Label — Inter, 600, UPPERCASE, 11-13px, letter-spacing 0.1em
     Example: "CONDITION: CRACKED SCREEN"

Price / Stat — Chakra Petch, 700, 32-64px
     Example: "£120.00"

Tag / Badge — Space Grotesk, 700, UPPERCASE, 11-12px
     Example: "EASY FIX" / "SPONSORED" / "DRIFT"
```

### Typography Rules

1. **H1s are always uppercase.** No exceptions. "Don't bin it. Earn from it." in sentence case is NOT BrokeyDokey. "DON'T BIN IT. EARN FROM IT." is BrokeyDokey.
2. **Prices always use Chakra Petch.** It's the "tech" font that makes numbers look serious.
3. **Don't mix more than two font families in a single asset.** Space Grotesk + Inter is the default pair. Chakra Petch is a special guest.
4. **Letter spacing on uppercase labels:** `0.1em` minimum. Cramped caps look amateur.
5. **Line height for body copy:** `1.6` (not `1.2`, which is for headlines only).

## 3. IMAGE STYLE GUIDE

### The BrokeyDokey Aesthetic

**In one sentence:** Real people, real damage, real workbenches. Not a stock photo agency. Not aspirational tech lifestyle. Authentic, slightly gritty, occasionally funny.

### What BrokeyDokey Photos Look Like

**Setting:**
- Workbenches (wood, metal, concrete — never white studio backdrops)
- Kitchen tables with tools scattered around
- Garage floors, workshops, dimly lit repair spaces
- The sofa where the thing got dropped (context is everything)
- No aspirational minimalist desks with a single device and a plant

**Lighting:**
- Natural window light when possible (creates honest, documentary feel)
- One practical lamp (bench lamp, angle-poise) for workshop shots
- Slightly underexposed is better than overlit and sterile
- Avoid: ring lights making tech look "influencer-perfect"

**Damage Documentation:**
- Get close. Show the crack. The bent port. The screen bleed.
- Don't cover damage or shoot around it — the damage IS the content
- Multiple angles: overall condition shot + close-up of specific damage
- If there's a funny damage detail (controller with a stick pointing 90 degrees sideways), that's your hero shot

**People (if in frame):**
- Hands are better than faces in most cases — universally relatable
- Show the work: soldering, unscrewing, diagnosing, plugging in
- People looking confused at a device is authentic and relatable
- Avoid: Posed "happy person looking at phone" stock photo energy

**Colour Treatment:**
- Real photos: slight desaturation (take it 15-20% towards greyscale in post) for editorial feel
- Product listings: greyscale until hover (replicate the in-app UX in ads)
- Campaign images: full colour, high contrast, with yellow accent overlays or frames

**What to Avoid:**
- White background product shots (this isn't a refurb site)
- Overly staged setups with matching props
- Smiling people who look like they've never touched a screwdriver
- Lens flares, bokeh so heavy the device is a blur
- Obvious stock photography (people will clock it immediately)

## 4. LOGO USAGE RULES

### Logo Versions
1. **Primary:** "BrokeyDokey" wordmark in Space Grotesk Bold, black text, yellow underline or yellow background block
2. **Reversed:** White wordmark on black background with yellow accent
3. **Yellow block:** Full yellow background, black text — for high-visibility use
4. **Minimal:** "BD" monogram in Chakra Petch for favicon, app icons, small-format use

### Logo Rules
1. **Clear space:** Minimum clear space = height of the "B" in the wordmark on all four sides
2. **Minimum size:** 120px wide for digital; 30mm for print
3. **Never:** Stretch, rotate, recolour (logo is black OR white, never coloured), add drop shadows, place on busy photographic backgrounds without a solid backing block
4. **Always:** Use on solid colour backgrounds (black, white, or BrokeyYellow) or with a backing rectangle
5. **Tagline pairing:** "DON'T BIN IT. EARN FROM IT." set in Space Grotesk 600 UPPERCASE appears below logo in formal brand usage (hero sections, press materials, merch)

### The Brutalist Shadow on Logo Elements
When the logo appears in a bordered box (e.g., header card, social profile image), use the standard brutalist shadow: `6px 6px 0px 0px #000000` on a white or yellow box.

## 5. VOICE AND TONE

### This Is Us (10 Examples)

1. "Your broken PS4 is worth more than your dignity. List it."
2. "Don't be a div. It takes 3 minutes to list."
3. "Someone out there wants your busted drone. Weird, but true."
4. "Your junk is their gold. Which is both exciting and slightly unsettling."
5. "The landfill doesn't want it either. Give it to someone who does."
6. "Broken stuff, meet people who fix stuff. It's practically romance."
7. "Every year, 23 million pieces of tech get binned in the UK. Some of it was definitely avoidable."
8. "AI just valued your broken laptop at £120. You were about to bin it for nothing, weren't you."
9. "The dog was judging you when you threw the controller. Now BrokeyDokey is too. List it."
10. "Protected by BrokeyGuard™ Escrow. Obvs."

### This Is NOT Us (10 Examples)

1. "Empower your sustainability journey with our innovative circular economy platform." (Corporate buzzword soup)
2. "We're passionate about making a difference for both people and planet!" (Beige.)
3. "Limited time offer! Don't miss out on this amazing deal!!!" (Multiple exclamation marks. Immediately binned.)
4. "Hi there! Just wanted to check in about your listing 😊" (Chatbot-chipper. Nobody is this enthusiastic about you.)
5. "BrokeyDokey: Because Every Device Deserves a Second Chance." (Fortune-cookie wisdom. Too soft.)
6. "We believe in the power of community to transform the future of technology." (Meaningless tech startup non-speak.)
7. "Congrats! You've joined the BrokeyDokey family!" (Not a family. A marketplace.)
8. "Your trusted partner for all your broken device selling needs." (Estate agent energy.)
9. "Let's make the world a better place, one device at a time 💚" (Climate grief marketing. Cloying.)
10. "As per our previous email, here is a reminder about your listing." (Legal department wrote this.)

### Tone Dial

```
[FORMAL]________________________[CASUAL]
                          ↑
                    BrokeyDokey
```

We sit at about 75% casual. We're not trying to be a Twitter comedian. We're trying to sound like a smart, dry friend who knows about tech and slightly despairs of the fact that you were going to bin a perfectly listable device.

## 6. CANVA TEMPLATE SPECS

### Social Image (1:1 — 1080x1080px)

**Layout A — "Listing Card":**
- Background: White
- 4px black border all around
- Top-left: Category tag (yellow fill, black border, Space Grotesk 600 UPPERCASE)
- Main: Product image (greyscale, 500x500 centre), 2px black border
- Bottom: Price in Chakra Petch 700, 40px / Item title in Space Grotesk 700, 24px
- Bottom-right corner: BrokeyDokey logo (reversed, white on black, 80px wide)
- Brutalist shadow on outer box: 6px 6px 0px 0px black (simulate with offset duplicate)

**Layout B — "Stat/Impact Post":**
- Background: BrokeyYellow (#E8457C)
- 4px black border
- Large number: Chakra Petch 800, 120px, black
- Subtext: Space Grotesk 600, UPPERCASE, 24px, black
- Bottom: White strip (height 120px) with tagline "DON'T BIN IT. EARN FROM IT."
- BrokeyDokey wordmark in black, bottom-right

**Layout C — "Quote/Copy Post":**
- Background: Black
- White or yellow text, Space Grotesk 700-800, UPPERCASE
- Single bold statement fills 80% of canvas
- Small logo in corner
- Optional: thin yellow border inset from edge (24px inset)

### YouTube Thumbnail (1280x720px)

- **Background:** Always high-contrast (black, yellow, or ScrapPink) — never a muddied photo
- **Text:** Space Grotesk 800, UPPERCASE, max 5 words, minimum 80px
- **Photo:** Person or device — must be large (takes up at least 40% of canvas)
- **Device photos:** Show the damage clearly. Crack, smoke damage, bent port = thumbnail gold.
- **Text border:** Black text gets white stroke (2px), white text gets black stroke (2px) — always legible on any background
- **Yellow accent:** At least one yellow element (price callout, tag, underline) in every thumbnail
- **Template folder in Canva:** "BD-YouTube-Thumbs" — 3 pre-built layouts (Device Close-Up / Person Reaction / Stat Reveal)

### Email Header (600x200px)

- **Background:** Black or BrokeyYellow
- **Logo:** Centred, white on black OR black on yellow, 200px wide
- **Tagline strip:** "DON'T BIN IT. EARN FROM IT." in Space Grotesk 600 UPPERCASE, 16px, letter-spacing 0.2em
- **No photography in email header** — too unreliable across email clients
- **Export as PNG at 2x (1200x400px)** then constrain width in email HTML

## 7. VIDEO AESTHETIC GUIDE

### Look and Feel

**The BrokeyDokey visual language for video:**

Deadpan British documentary meets product film. Think early Louis Theroux more than tech ad. The broken stuff is the star. The humans are embarrassed bystanders.

### Lighting

- **Repair workshop content:** Practical light sources only (bench lamp, window). One hard light source, one fill bounce (white foam board). Shadows are acceptable — they add authenticity.
- **Talking head (founder, reviewer):** Key light at 45 degrees to face, fill at 1/2 power opposite, hair light optional. Background: blurred workshop OR solid colour (yellow, black) for cleaner content.
- **Product macro (damage close-ups):** Diffused overhead light + side directional light to pick out texture, cracks, surface damage. A busted screen looks terrible in flat light. Side-light it.
- **Avoid:** Ring light (too smooth, too "YouTube beauty"), overhead fluorescent (colour sickly green), bare LED strip (harsh and ugly)

### Colour Grade

**Phase 1 — Reckoning scenes (broken device, sad moment):**
- Pull saturation to -25% to -35%
- Lift contrast (+15)
- Cool the shadows (blue tint, subtle)
- This is: "It's broken. Life has little colour."

**Phase 2 — Discovery/BrokeyDokey moment:**
- Saturation returns to 0% (neutral)
- Warm the highlights (golden +5 on hue)
- This is the visual turn: "Something is changing."

**Phase 3 — Post-sale/repair montage:**
- Full saturation (+10% from neutral)
- Warm grade overall (cinematic golden-hour feel)
- Contrast remains high
- This is: "The device has a new life. So do you. Mildly."

**For Shorts/Reels only:**
- Full colour throughout (no desaturation)
- Higher contrast (+20)
- Punchy, fast, immediate. Subtlety is for 15-minute YouTube essays.

### Music Vibe

- **Launch film / brand hero:** Orchestral drama → pivot at 0:25 to indie-electronic (think: Mac DeMarco energy, slightly deadpan, not aspirational)
- **Repair tutorial:** Lo-fi beats to fix to. Background only. Never distracting.
- **Roast My Junk:** Game show energy. Light percussion, slightly goofy horn stab on reveals.
- **Founder updates / talking head:** Silent or very quiet acoustic guitar. Let the content speak.
- **Shorts/Reels:** Trending audio via CapCut — always check what's ranking in UK this week. Swap it out every 2 weeks.

**Music sources (royalty-free, safe for YouTube monetisation):**
- Artlist.io (£159/year — unlimited downloads, YouTube/social safe)
- Epidemic Sound (£12/mo — great for lo-fi, indie, electronic)
- YouTube Audio Library (free — limited selection but zero risk)

### Editing Rules

- **Cut on action:** Cut when something moves (hand picks up device, screwdriver turns). Never cut in dead air.
- **Jump cuts:** Acceptable in tutorial content. Unacceptable in brand hero films.
- **On-screen text:** Space Grotesk 700, UPPERCASE, yellow or white, hard-cut onto screen (no slide-in animations). "SLAP" the text on. Brutalist = no fancy transitions.
- **Captions:** Auto-captions (Descript or CapCut) edited for accuracy. White text, black background, always on. 85% of Reels plays are muted.
- **Aspect ratio exports:** 16:9 main → 9:16 vertical reframe → 1:1 square crop. Plan framing during shoot (keep faces and key action centre-frame for flexible reframing).
